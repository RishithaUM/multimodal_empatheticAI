🎚️ Audio Features Extracted

  1. Energy (RMS): Overall volume/intensity of speech
  2. Pitch (ZCR): How many times signal crosses zero per second
  3. Spectral Centroid: "Brightness" of the sound
  4. Tempo: Speaking rate (bursts per second)
energy = √(Σ samples² / N) × 1000 pitch = zero_crossings / length centroid = Σ(frequency × magnitude) / Σ(magnitude)

💻 Technology Stack

  • MediaRecorder API: Captures audio stream
  • Web Audio API: Decodes and analyzes audio
  • AudioContext: Processes raw audio data
  • AnalyserNode: Real-time frequency analysis
  • FFT (Fast Fourier Transform): Size 256 for speech

🧭 Emotion Mapping Rules

😄 EXCITED: High Energy (>70) + High Pitch (>200 Hz)
💡 Indicates: Enthusiasm, high energy, positive excitement
Confidence: 75-90%
😊 HAPPY: Medium Energy (>50) + Medium Pitch (>150 Hz)
💡 Indicates: Pleasure, contentment, positive mood
Confidence: 65-85%
😢 SAD: Low Energy (<30) + Low Pitch (<120 Hz)
💡 Indicates: Sadness, depression, low mood
Confidence: 60-80%
😰 ANXIOUS: High Spectral Centroid (>2000 Hz)
💡 Indicates: Tension, worry, stress, nervousness
Confidence: 55-75%
😐 NEUTRAL/CALM: Balanced Features
💡 Indicates: Normal speech, relaxed, peaceful
Confidence: 55-70%

📊 Why These Features Work

  • Energy: Excited/happy people speak louder; sad people speak softly
  • Pitch: Higher pitch indicates positive emotions; lower pitch indicates sad/calm emotions
  • Spectral Centroid: Anxious/tense speech has more high-frequency content
  • Speaking Rate: Excited people speak faster; depressed people speak slower
  • Combination: Multiple features together give more accurate classification
